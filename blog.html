Introducing the Pittsburgh Rental Market Map

I've built an interactive rental market map for Pittsburgh investors, covering about 180 neighborhoods and towns across the city and surrounding suburbs. Each area is outlined and shaded by rent, and you can switch between apartments, houses and small multifamily units at every common bedroom count. Hover over any neighborhood to see its rent range instantly. The ranges start from published rent data and are refined with a simple demand test: listings getting about one inquiry a day are priced right. The estimates are AI-generated, so treat them as a strong starting point, not investment advice, and verify with current comps before you buy. The map isn't public, so if you'd like access, book a 30-minute call with me. I'll walk you through it and send you the passcode to view at anytime.
