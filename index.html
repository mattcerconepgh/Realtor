West End Bridge Connector

Esplanade: North Side riverfront redevelopment

A roughly 15-acre brownfield site on Pittsburgh's North Side is being redeveloped into a mixed-use riverfront district with apartments, condos, a hotel, retail, and public green space. Site work and phase one construction are targeted for 2025 to 2028.

Work With A Pittsburgh Investment Specialist

If you're serious about investing here, you need more than listings. You need deal flow, market insight, and someone who can execute while you're not in the room. Most of my clients are out-of-state and rely on me to identify, underwrite, and close deals on their behalf.

How I Help Out-of-State Investors

  • Identify deals that fit your buy box
  • Analyze properties with real numbers and local insight
  • Walk properties and give you honest feedback
  • Coordinate inspections, contractors, and due diligence
  • Negotiate and manage deals through closing

Most of my clients are not local. The process is built so you can invest confidently without needing to be physically present.

Most investors I work with buy their first Pittsburgh property without ever visiting in person.
