MC

I'm an investment focused agent and real estate investor helping buyers acquire, sell, and scale across Pittsburgh — single-family homes, multifamily, and small commercial.

As an active rental property owner myself, I underwrite deals the way an investor does: rent comps first, rehab budget second, offer price last. That's the same order I walk every client through, whether it's a first duplex or a fifth property in a growing portfolio.
